> **In plain terms:** The UAE has several crypto regulators. Dubai has its own, and two financial free zones each have one. There is also a federal markets regulator, plus the central bank for stablecoins and payments. A crypto firm must get the licence that matches where and what it operates. Individuals pay no income tax on crypto gains, and trading crypto is exempt from VAT, but crypto businesses can owe corporate tax.

The United Arab Emirates regulates **virtual assets** through overlapping authorities. Which one applies depends on the emirate, any financial free zone involved, and the activity.[^chambers] Dubai created a dedicated regulator, the **Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority** (VARA), in 2022.[^vara][^chambers] On 1 January 2026 a new federal **Capital Market Authority** (CMA) replaced the Securities and Commodities Authority (SCA), with virtual assets inside its remit.[^dechert][^horizons]

The UAE has no personal income tax.[^pwc-pit] Since 15 November 2024, transfers and conversions of virtual assets have been exempt from value added tax (VAT), with effect back to 2018.[^bm-vat]

## Regulation

### Who regulates what

| Area | Regulator | Main rules |
|---|---|---|
| Dubai, outside the DIFC | VARA | Law No. 4 of 2022; VARA regulations and rulebooks[^vara][^chambers] |
| Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM) free zone | Financial Services Regulatory Authority (FSRA) | ADGM's own framework[^chambers] |
| Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC) free zone | Dubai Financial Services Authority (DFSA) | DIFC's own framework[^chambers] |
| Rest of the UAE, and federal matters | Capital Market Authority (CMA) | Decree-Laws No. 32 and 33 of 2025; Decision No. 4/R.M/2026[^dechert][^horizons] |
| Payment tokens (stablecoins) and payment-related DeFi | Central Bank of the UAE (CBUAE) | Payment Token Services Regulation; Decree-Law No. 6 of 2025[^pnyx] |

### VARA (Dubai)

VARA licenses virtual-asset activities in Dubai, except inside the DIFC.[^chambers] It issues activity-specific rulebooks, for example for exchanges, brokers, custodians and token issuers. In February 2026 it said tokenised Dubai property falls under its rules.[^sumsub]

### The federal Capital Market Authority

**Federal Decree-Law No. 32 of 2025** made the CMA the legal successor of the SCA from 1 January 2026.[^dechert] **Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2025** rewrote licensing for financial activities and added virtual assets to the definition of financial products.[^dechert] The law also reaches firms outside the UAE that target clients inside it.[^dechert] Unlicensed financial activity can be punished by at least a year in prison and fines of up to AED 250 million.[^dechert]

**CMA Decision No. 4/R.M/2026** replaced the SCA's earlier virtual asset rules.[^horizons] It defines eight licensed activities, including dealing, custody, operating a trading facility and portfolio management.[^horizons] Platforms must assess tokens and register them with the CMA before offering them. Approved tokens go on a CMA "Green List".[^horizons] The decision bans activities involving privacy tokens and algorithmic tokens. Utility tokens and NFTs are banned with limited exceptions.[^horizons] It works alongside VARA and does not apply in the ADGM or DIFC.[^horizons]

### The central bank: payment tokens and DeFi

The CBUAE's **Payment Token Services Regulation** of 2024 requires authorisation for issuers of dirham-backed tokens. Foreign stablecoins may be used in the UAE only to pay for virtual assets. It bans algorithmic stablecoins and privacy tokens as means of payment.[^pnyx]

**Federal Decree-Law No. 6 of 2025**, the new Central Bank law, took effect on 16 September 2025.[^pnyx] It applies licensing to anyone who carries out or facilitates a licensed financial activity "by any medium or technology". Commentators read this as covering decentralised finance (DeFi) platforms and applications.[^pnyx] Firms had about one year to comply.[^pnyx]

## Taxation

### Individuals

There is "currently no personal income tax" in the UAE, and no individual tax registration or reporting.[^pwc-pit] Individuals who conduct a business in the UAE can fall under corporate tax once their turnover passes a threshold.[^pwc-pit][^mof-ct]

### Businesses

Federal **corporate tax** applies to financial years starting on or after 1 June 2023.[^mof-ct] The rate is 9% on taxable income above AED 375,000, and 0% below that.[^pwc-cit] Crypto businesses, such as exchanges and trading companies, are taxable persons like other UAE companies.[^mof-ct]

### VAT

Following Cabinet Decision No. 100 of 2024, the VAT Executive Regulation was amended from 15 November 2024. Transfers and conversions of virtual assets became exempt.[^bm-vat] These exemptions are treated as effective from 1 January 2018.[^bm-vat] Digital forms of fiat currency and financial securities are excluded from the "virtual asset" definition.[^bm-vat]

## Criticism and open debates

- **Overlapping regulators.** With five authorities, firms may need more than one licence. Commentators note the CMA's listing powers could limit which tokens trade even on VARA-licensed platforms.[^pnyx-cma]
- **Unsettled boundaries.** As of early 2026 the CMA had not issued guidance on cross-border activity, despite the law's reach to firms targeting UAE clients.[^dechert]
- **DeFi.** Applying licensing to DeFi "by any medium or technology" is broad, and its practical enforcement is untested.[^pnyx]

## Practical notes

- Check which regulator's register covers a platform: VARA in Dubai, FSRA in ADGM, DFSA in the DIFC, or the CMA elsewhere.[^chambers]
- Dollar stablecoins may be used to buy virtual assets, but not as general means of payment in the UAE.[^pnyx]
- Tax treatment depends on whether activity amounts to a business; see the Ministry of Finance's guidance.[^mof-ct]
- This article is general information, not tax or legal advice.
