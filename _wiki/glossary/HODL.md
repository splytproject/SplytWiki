## In detail

HODL describes a long-term holding strategy. It is often used to encourage others not to sell in a panic, but holding an asset is a choice with its own risks, not a guarantee of gains.

## Word origin

From a misspelling of *hold* in a 2013 Bitcoin Forum post titled "I AM HODLING".[^hodl] The expansion "hold on for dear life" was invented later.
