## In detail

Victims are shown fake profits, then asked for "fees" or "taxes" before they can withdraw.[^fincen] Many such scams are run from compounds that use trafficked workers.

## Word origin

A translation of the Chinese *sha zhu pan*: the victim is "fattened" before being "slaughtered".[^fincen]
