This **crypto glossary** explains language commonly encountered in cryptocurrency articles, applications and online communities. Slang is informal and context-dependent; an enthusiastic slogan does not establish that a project is useful or that an investment will succeed. Technical terms are grouped by subject so readers can compare related ideas.

For a first reading, start with *blockchain*, *wallet*, *private key*, *transaction*, *gas* and *token*. For practical account protection, see [cryptocurrency wallets](../Cryptocurrency_wallet/). The tables below give quick explanations. **Select any term to open its own entry**, with fuller definitions, example sentences, related terms and a link to the main article.

## Community slang

| Term | Plain-language meaning | Context or caution |
|---|---|---|
| **[HODL](../glossary/HODL/)** | Hold an asset rather than sell during volatility | Originated in a misspelled 2013 forum post; “hold on for dear life” is a later reinterpretation.[^hodl] |
| **[WAGMI](../glossary/WAGMI/)** | “We're all going to make it” | A statement of group optimism, not a promise of returns |
| **[NGMI](../glossary/NGMI/)** | “Not going to make it” | Often a dismissive joke; it is not meaningful analysis |
| **[GM](../glossary/GM/)** | “Good morning” | A common community greeting |
| **[FOMO](../glossary/FOMO/)** | Fear of missing out | Can encourage decisions based on urgency instead of evidence |
| **[FUD](../glossary/FUD/)** | Fear, uncertainty and doubt | Sometimes used to dismiss valid criticism as well as rumours |
| **[DYOR](../glossary/DYOR/)** | Do your own research | A slogan that does not excuse misleading promotion |
| **[NFA](../glossary/NFA/)** | Not financial advice | A disclaimer that does not establish the accuracy of a claim |
| **[Rekt](../glossary/Rekt/)** | Suffered a severe loss | Informal spelling of “wrecked” |
| **[Whale](../glossary/Whale/)** | A holder large enough to matter in a particular market | No universal token amount defines a whale |
| **[Diamond hands](../glossary/Diamond_hands/)** | Someone determined to keep holding | Persistence can preserve either a good position or a bad one |
| **[Paper hands](../glossary/Paper_hands/)** | Someone described as selling quickly | Often social pressure rather than a useful description of risk |
| **[Bagholder](../glossary/Bagholder/)** | Someone left holding an asset after a large decline | The term says nothing about future recovery |
| **[Moon](../glossary/Moon/)** | A hoped-for dramatic price rise | “To the moon” expresses enthusiasm |
| **[ATH](../glossary/ATH/)** | All-time high: the highest price an asset has ever reached | A past peak says nothing about future prices; "ATL" is the all-time low |
| **[Pump](../glossary/Pump/)** | A rapid rise in price | Can result from ordinary demand or organized manipulation |
| **[Dump](../glossary/Dump/)** | Heavy selling or a rapid price decline | Context is needed to identify the cause |
| **[Shill](../glossary/Shill/)** | Promote an asset enthusiastically, often with an undisclosed interest | Check incentives and supporting evidence |
| **[Degen](../glossary/Degen/)** | A participant embracing unusually speculative activity | A community label, not a measure of expertise |
| **[Rug pull](../glossary/Rug_pull/)** | Project insiders extract value or abandon a promoted venture to holders' detriment | Removing liquidity is one possible form; a falling price alone does not prove fraud.[^rug] |

## Assets and ownership

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **[Cryptocurrency](../glossary/Cryptocurrency/)** | A digital asset system using cryptography and a distributed ledger for ownership and transfers |
| **[Coin](../glossary/Coin/)** | Usually the native asset of a blockchain, although everyday usage is inconsistent |
| **[Altcoin](../glossary/Altcoin/)** | A cryptocurrency other than Bitcoin |
| **[Token](../glossary/Token/)** | An asset represented on a network, often by a smart contract |
| **[Satoshi](../glossary/Satoshi/)** | The smallest unit of bitcoin: 0.00000001 BTC, one hundred-millionth of a coin, named after [Satoshi Nakamoto](../Satoshi_Nakamoto/)[^supply] |
| **[White paper](../glossary/White_paper/)** | A document in which a project describes its design and goals, such as the 2008 [Bitcoin](../Bitcoin/) white paper; it is a description, not an audit or a guarantee[^whitepaper] |
| **[Fungible](../glossary/Fungible/)** | Interchangeable with another unit of the same kind |
| **[NFT](../glossary/NFT/)** | A non-fungible token distinguished from other tokens by its identifier; ownership does not automatically grant copyright |
| **[Stablecoin](../glossary/Stablecoin/)** | A token designed to track a reference value, such as one dollar |
| **[Peg](../glossary/Peg/)** | The target exchange value an asset aims to maintain |
| **[Depeg](../glossary/Depeg/)** | A meaningful departure from that target value |
| **[Governance token](../glossary/Governance_token/)** | A token associated with participation in specified decisions |
| **[Utility token](../glossary/Utility_token/)** | A token with a stated service or access function |
| **[Meme coin](../glossary/Meme_coin/)** | An asset associated primarily with a joke, cultural theme or community identity |
| **[Mint](../glossary/Mint/)** | Create new units under an asset's rules |
| **[Burn](../glossary/Burn/)** | Remove units from circulation through a defined mechanism |
| **[Airdrop](../glossary/Airdrop/)** | A token distribution to selected addresses; an unsolicited token can also be bait for a scam |
| **[Tokenomics](../glossary/Tokenomics/)** | The design of token supply, distribution and incentives |

See [altcoins and tokens](../Altcoins_and_tokens/), [NFT standards](../NFT_standards/) and [stablecoins](../Stablecoin/) for the distinctions behind these labels.

## Networks and execution

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **[Blockchain](../glossary/Blockchain/)** | A shared record organized into linked blocks |
| **[Block](../glossary/Block/)** | A group of recorded transactions and associated network data |
| **[Node](../glossary/Node/)** | A computer participating in a network by checking, storing or relaying information |
| **[Consensus](../glossary/Consensus/)** | Rules and processes by which participants agree on accepted history |
| **[Validator](../glossary/Validator/)** | A participant performing protocol duties such as proposing or attesting to blocks |
| **[UTXO](../glossary/UTXO/)** | Unspent transaction output: a spendable piece of bitcoin; a Bitcoin balance is the sum of the UTXOs a wallet can spend[^devglossary] |
| **[Mempool](../glossary/Mempool/)** | The waiting area where a node keeps valid transactions that are not yet in a block[^devglossary] |
| **[Mining](../glossary/Mining/)** | Performing proof-of-work computations to compete in block production |
| **[Hash rate](../glossary/Hash_rate/)** | The total computing power working on [proof of work](../Proof_of_work/), measured in hashes per second |
| **[Halving](../glossary/Halving/)** | The scheduled cut, every 210,000 blocks (about four years), in the new bitcoin paid to miners per block[^supply] |
| **[51% attack](../glossary/51_percent_attack/)** | An attempt by whoever controls most of a network's mining power (or stake) to rewrite recent history, for example to spend the same coins twice[^whitepaper] |
| **[Staking](../glossary/Staking/)** | Committing assets under a proof-of-stake system's rules; commercial products use the word more broadly |
| **[Slashing](../glossary/Slashing/)** | A protocol penalty that removes some staked assets for specified violations |
| **[Transaction](../glossary/Transaction/)** | A submitted instruction that can change network state |
| **[Confirmation](../glossary/Confirmation/)** | Inclusion of a transaction in accepted history; stronger assurance can require additional steps |
| **[Finality](../glossary/Finality/)** | The point at which reversal is prevented or becomes exceptionally unlikely under a network's assumptions |
| **[Fork](../glossary/Fork/)** | A divergence in rules or chain history |
| **[Gas](../glossary/Gas/)** | A unit measuring execution work on networks such as [Ethereum](../Ethereum/)[^gas] |
| **[Gas fee](../glossary/Gas_fee/)** | The amount paid for processing a transaction, determined by work and the applicable price |
| **[Gwei](../glossary/Gwei/)** | One billionth of an ether |
| **[Smart contract](../glossary/Smart_contract/)** | Blockchain code that processes inputs according to programmed rules |
| **[DApp](../glossary/DApp/)** | A decentralized application that uses network-based backend logic |
| **[Oracle](../glossary/Oracle/)** | A mechanism supplying external information to blockchain programs |
| **[MEV](../glossary/MEV/)** | Maximal extractable value: profit a block producer or bot can make by including, excluding or reordering transactions, for example by front-running a large trade[^mev] |
| **[Testnet](../glossary/Testnet/)** | A separate network for testing software, whose coins have no market value by design |
| **[Faucet](../glossary/Faucet/)** | A website that hands out free testnet coins for development; a "faucet" offering real coins is usually a scam |

The [Ethereum glossary](https://ethereum.org/en/glossary/) provides further technical terminology;[^ethglossary] explanations of execution are in [smart contracts](../Smart_contract/).

## Scaling and moving between networks

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **[Layer 1 (L1)](../glossary/Layer_1/)** | A base blockchain with its own consensus and transaction history |
| **[Layer 2 (L2)](../glossary/Layer_2/)** | A system scaling a base network under a defined settlement and security relationship |
| **[Rollup](../glossary/Rollup/)** | A system executing transactions outside a base chain and submitting data and commitments or proofs to it |
| **[Optimistic rollup](../glossary/Optimistic_rollup/)** | A rollup using a dispute process to challenge invalid execution claims |
| **[ZK rollup](../glossary/ZK_rollup/)** | A rollup using validity proofs; the name does not inherently imply private transactions |
| **[Sidechain](../glossary/Sidechain/)** | A separate blockchain connected to another network but using its own consensus |
| **[Bridge](../glossary/Bridge/)** | A mechanism transferring messages or representations of assets between networks |
| **[Sequencer](../glossary/Sequencer/)** | A component that orders transactions in a system such as a rollup |
| **[Data availability](../glossary/Data_availability/)** | Whether information needed to verify or reconstruct state can be obtained |
| **[Throughput](../glossary/Throughput/)** | The amount of processing completed in a period; measurement methods matter |

See [blockchain scaling](../Blockchain_scaling/) for tradeoffs that these short definitions cannot fully express.

## Wallets and security

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **[Address](../glossary/Address/)** | An identifier used to receive assets or interact with an account |
| **[Public key](../glossary/Public_key/)** | Cryptographic information used to verify signatures |
| **[Private key](../glossary/Private_key/)** | A secret enabling transaction authorization in a basic key-controlled account |
| **[Seed phrase](../glossary/Seed_phrase/)** | Recovery words that can derive a wallet's underlying secrets; never share them (see [Seed phrases and self-custody security](../Seed_phrases_and_self-custody_security/)) |
| **[Signature](../glossary/Signature/)** | Cryptographic authorization of a message or transaction |
| **[Custody](../glossary/Custody/)** | Control and safekeeping of assets or their signing authority |
| **[Self-custody](../glossary/Self-custody/)** | An arrangement in which the user controls authorization rather than relying on a custodian |
| **[Hot wallet](../glossary/Hot_wallet/)** | A wallet whose signing secrets are held in an internet-connected environment |
| **[Cold storage](../glossary/Cold_storage/)** | Keeping signing secrets offline |
| **[Hardware wallet](../glossary/Hardware_wallet/)** | A dedicated device designed to protect keys and sign internally |
| **[Multisig](../glossary/Multisig/)** | An authorization scheme requiring several signatures |
| **[Approval](../glossary/Approval/)** | Permission for a specified contract or party to spend tokens under defined conditions |
| **[Phishing](../glossary/Phishing/)** | Deception intended to obtain secrets or harmful authorization |
| **[Pig butchering](../glossary/Pig_butchering/)** | A long-con scam in which a fraudster builds a friendship or romance, then steers the victim into a fake crypto investment platform[^fincen] |
| **[Address poisoning](../glossary/Address_poisoning/)** | Planting a lookalike address in records to encourage a mistaken transfer |

Scams often combine these techniques; the US Federal Trade Commission lists common warning signs, such as guaranteed returns and demands to pay in crypto.[^ftc] A wallet password, device PIN and seed phrase serve different purposes. A password reset cannot ordinarily replace a missing self-custody recovery secret. See [wallet security](../Cryptocurrency_wallet/).

## Markets, DeFi and governance

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **[Bull market](../glossary/Bull_market/)** | A broadly rising market period |
| **[Bear market](../glossary/Bear_market/)** | A broadly declining market period |
| **[Volatility](../glossary/Volatility/)** | The degree of price fluctuation |
| **[Liquidity](../glossary/Liquidity/)** | The ability to trade without greatly changing price |
| **[Spread](../glossary/Spread/)** | The difference between quoted buying and selling prices |
| **[Slippage](../glossary/Slippage/)** | The difference between an expected execution price and the price obtained |
| **[Market capitalization](../glossary/Market_capitalization/)** | Price multiplied by circulating supply |
| **[FDV](../glossary/FDV/)** | Fully diluted valuation, using an assumed expanded supply |
| **[Leverage](../glossary/Leverage/)** | Exposure enlarged through borrowing or derivatives |
| **[Liquidation](../glossary/Liquidation/)** | Forced closing or collateral sale under borrowing rules |
| **[Collateral](../glossary/Collateral/)** | Assets pledged to support an obligation |
| **[CEX](../glossary/CEX/)** | Centralized exchange: a company that holds customers' funds and matches trades, unlike a DEX; usually subject to [KYC](../KYC_and_AML/) |
| **[ETF](../glossary/ETF/)** | Exchange-traded fund: a fund traded like a share; US spot bitcoin ETFs were approved in January 2024, giving exposure without holding keys[^etf] |
| **[DeFi](../glossary/DeFi/)** | Financial services implemented through blockchain applications |
| **[DEX](../glossary/DEX/)** | A decentralized exchange using network-based trading rules |
| **[Liquidity pool](../glossary/Liquidity_pool/)** | Assets deposited into a contract to support trading or another service |
| **[Impermanent loss](../glossary/Impermanent_loss/)** | The shortfall a [liquidity pool](../Decentralized_finance/) depositor can suffer, compared with simply holding the tokens, when their prices move apart |
| **[Yield](../glossary/Yield/)** | A return measured over time; its source and risks need examination |
| **[APY](../glossary/APY/)** | Annual percentage yield including an assumed compounding effect |
| **[DAO](../glossary/DAO/)** | An organization coordinating decisions through shared governance arrangements, often using blockchain tools |
| **[Quorum](../glossary/Quorum/)** | A minimum participation requirement for a valid decision |
| **[Delegation](../glossary/Delegation/)** | Assigning voting power or another defined responsibility to a representative |
| **[Treasury](../glossary/Treasury/)** | Assets held for an organization's activities |
| **[Timelock](../glossary/Timelock/)** | A programmed delay before an action becomes executable |

## Compliance vocabulary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **[KYC](../glossary/KYC/)** | Know Your Customer: identifying and verifying a customer |
| **[AML](../glossary/AML/)** | Anti-money laundering: controls addressing the movement of criminal proceeds |
| **[CFT](../glossary/CFT/)** | Countering the financing of terrorism |
| **[VASP](../glossary/VASP/)** | Virtual asset service provider, a category used in international AML standards |
| **[Travel Rule](../glossary/Travel_Rule/)** | Requirements for covered providers to transmit specified transfer-related information |
| **[Cost basis](../glossary/Cost_basis/)** | The acquisition value used when calculating taxable gains under applicable rules |
| **[Disposal](../glossary/Disposal/)** | Giving up an asset through a transaction such as a sale or exchange; tax meaning depends on jurisdiction |
