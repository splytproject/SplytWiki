> **In plain terms:** In the UK, selling or swapping crypto can trigger Capital Gains Tax, and crypto received as income is taxed as income. Crypto firms currently need to register with the Financial Conduct Authority for money-laundering checks and follow strict advertising rules. From October 2027 they will need full FCA authorisation, like banks and brokers.

The United Kingdom taxes cryptoassets under its existing **capital gains** and **income tax** rules, as explained in HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) guidance.[^hmrc-sell][^hmrc-manual] Regulation is led by the **Financial Conduct Authority** (FCA). Since January 2020 the FCA has supervised crypto businesses for anti-money laundering (AML). Since October 2023 it has applied rules to crypto advertising.[^fca-aml][^fca-ps23-6]

A comprehensive regime is being phased in. The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Cryptoassets) Regulations 2026 (SI 2026/102) were made on 4 February 2026.[^ls-si] The FCA published final rules on 30 June 2026. The new regime is expected to take effect on **25 October 2027**.[^fca-regime]

## Taxation

### Capital Gains Tax

For most individuals, cryptoassets are capital assets. **Capital Gains Tax** (CGT) may be due when they sell, swap, spend or give away tokens.[^hmrc-sell] Gifts to a spouse, civil partner or charity are exempt.[^hmrc-sell]

- **Allowance.** Total gains above the annual exempt amount are taxable. For 2026–27 the amount is **£3,000**.[^govuk-cgt]
- **Rates.** Gains within the basic income-tax band are taxed at **18%**; gains above it at **24%**.[^govuk-cgt]
- **Reporting.** Gains above the allowance must be reported to HMRC and the tax paid.[^hmrc-sell]

### Matching and pooling

HMRC does not let investors pick which coins they sold. Tokens of each type are matched in a fixed order:[^hmrc-pool]

1. **Same-day rule.** Disposals are first matched with tokens of the same type bought that day (section 105 of the Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992).
2. **30-day rule.** Next, with tokens bought in the following 30 days ("bed and breakfasting").
3. **Section 104 pool.** Remaining tokens come from a pool. Its cost is the average of all earlier purchases.

The 30-day rule stops people selling and quickly rebuying to realise a loss for tax.[^hmrc-sell][^hmrc-pool]

### Income Tax

Tokens received as payment, mining rewards or staking rewards may be **income**. Income Tax and, for employees, National Insurance can apply.[^hmrc-staking] HMRC's manual says staking rewards are generally taxed as miscellaneous income, or as trading income if staking amounts to a trade. Their value counts at the time they are received.[^hmrc-staking] A later disposal of the same tokens can then give rise to CGT.[^hmrc-staking]

### Reporting by platforms: CARF

The UK has adopted the OECD's **Cryptoasset Reporting Framework** (CARF). From 1 January 2026, UK crypto service providers must collect customers' details and transactions and report them to HMRC.[^govuk-carf] HMRC can share the data with other countries' tax authorities.[^govuk-carf]

## Regulation

### Anti-money laundering registration

Since 10 January 2020, UK cryptoasset exchange and custodian-wallet businesses must register with the FCA under the Money Laundering Regulations.[^fca-aml] They must verify customers and monitor transactions.[^fca-aml] Registration is not authorisation, and does not mean the FCA protects customers' money.

### Financial promotions

Since 8 October 2023, adverts for cryptoassets aimed at UK consumers must be approved or issued by an authorised or registered firm.[^fca-ps23-6] Cryptoassets are classed as "Restricted Mass Market Investments". Rules include risk warnings, a 24-hour cooling-off period for first-time investors, and appropriateness checks.[^fca-ps23-6] Incentives to invest, such as referral bonuses, are banned.[^fca-ps23-6]

### Investment products

In January 2021 the FCA banned the sale of crypto derivatives and ETNs to retail consumers.[^crs-cetn] From 8 October 2025 retail investors may buy crypto ETNs listed on FCA-recognised exchanges.[^crs-cetn] These are not covered by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme. The retail ban on crypto derivatives remains.[^crs-cetn][^rt-cetn]

### The new FSMA regime

The Cryptoassets Regulations 2026 bring cryptoasset activities into the same authorisation system as other financial services.[^ls-si] New regulated activities include running a trading platform, dealing, safeguarding (custody), staking, and issuing qualifying stablecoins in the UK.[^fca-regime][^fca-ps26-18]

The FCA's final rules cover:[^fca-regime]

- token admissions, disclosures and **market abuse** (PS26/9);
- **stablecoin** issuance (PS26/10);
- conduct for regulated activities (PS26/11);
- **prudential** capital and liquidity (PS26/12);
- how the wider FCA Handbook applies (PS26/13).

Firms wishing to operate when the regime starts must apply between 30 September 2026 and 28 February 2027.[^fca-regime] Firms that apply in time but are still being assessed in October 2027 enter a "saving period" and can keep serving customers.[^ls-si] Work on decentralised finance, derivatives and some stablecoin questions was still ongoing in September 2026.[^fca-ps26-18]

## Criticism and open debates

- **Unfinished areas.** As of September 2026 the FCA was still working on decentralised finance, crypto derivatives and parts of the stablecoin regime.[^fca-ps26-18]
- **Consumer protection.** The FCA's required risk warning tells consumers not to invest unless they are prepared to lose all the money they invest. AML registration does not mean the FCA protects customers' funds.[^fca-ps23-6][^fca-aml]
- **Retail access.** Allowing retail crypto ETNs widened access, but they carry no Financial Services Compensation Scheme protection.[^crs-cetn]
- **Record-keeping.** Pooling rules require a record of every transaction and of each pool's cost.[^hmrc-pool]

## Practical notes

- Keep a record of every acquisition and disposal: date, number of tokens, value in pounds, and fees.[^hmrc-sell]
- Moving tokens between your own wallets is not a disposal, but it can complicate records.[^hmrc-sell]
- Under CARF, expect platforms to ask for your tax residence and identity details.[^govuk-carf]
- This article is general information, not tax or legal advice.
