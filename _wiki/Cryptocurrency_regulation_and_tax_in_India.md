> **In plain terms:** Owning or trading crypto is legal in India, but heavily taxed. Profits are taxed at a flat 30%, losses cannot reduce other tax, and 1% is withheld on most sales. Exchanges serving Indian users must register with India's anti-money-laundering agency. There is still no full crypto law, and the central bank remains opposed to private cryptocurrencies.

India regulates cryptocurrency mainly through **tax law** and **anti-money-laundering (AML) law**, without a dedicated statute. The law calls crypto-assets and similar tokens **virtual digital assets** (VDAs).[^ita-2025][^schedule-vda] Since 2022, VDA income has been taxed at a flat 30%, with no relief for losses.[^schedule-vda] Since March 2023, VDA service providers have been reporting entities under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002 (PMLA).[^pib-2023]

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) tried to cut crypto off from the banking system in 2018. The Supreme Court struck that down in 2020.[^rbi-2018][^sc-2020] As of October 2026, the government has not decided between a ban and a regulatory framework.[^ct-rbi-2026]

## Taxation

### What counts as a VDA

The definition, introduced by the Finance Act, 2022 as section 2(47A) of the 1961 Act, covers crypto-assets, NFTs and similar tokens. Indian rupees and foreign currencies are excluded.[^schedule-vda] The Income-tax Act, 2025 (Act No. 30 of 2025) carries the definition forward. It received assent on 21 August 2025 and took effect on 1 April 2026.[^ita-2025-wiki]

### The 30% tax

Income from transferring a VDA is taxed at **30%**, plus surcharge and cess. This was section 115BBH of the 1961 Act.[^schedule-vda] Under the 2025 Act it is entry 4 of the table in section 194(1).[^ita-2025]

The rules are strict:[^ita-2025][^schedule-vda]

- **Only cost of acquisition is deductible.** Fees, interest and other expenses cannot be deducted.
- **No loss offset.** A loss on one VDA cannot be set off against any other income.
- **No carry-forward.** VDA losses cannot be carried to later years.
- **Flat rate.** The 30% applies whatever the holding period or the taxpayer's income.

### The 1% TDS

Tax deducted at source (TDS) means the payer withholds part of a payment and sends it to the government. On transfers of a VDA to a resident, the payer must withhold **1%**. This was section 194S of the 1961 Act, from 1 July 2022.[^cbdt-13]

No deduction is needed below ₹50,000 a year for "specified persons", such as individuals without business income, or ₹10,000 for others.[^cbdt-13] CBDT Circular No. 13 of 2022 explains who deducts on exchange trades: the exchange, or both the exchange and the broker.[^cbdt-13] From April 2026 the obligation sits in the new Act's consolidated TDS section, section 393.[^ita-2025-tds]

### Reporting

Individuals report VDA income in **Schedule VDA** of the income-tax return. Each transfer is listed with dates, cost and sale value.[^schedule-vda] From 1 April 2026, section 285BAA, added by the Finance Act, 2025, requires prescribed entities to report crypto-asset transactions to the tax department.[^285baa]

### Criticism

Exchanges and industry groups argue the 30% rate, the 1% TDS and the ban on loss offset push traders to offshore platforms.[^ifc-2026] The Income Tax Department has raised the opposite concern: that offshore exchanges and private wallets are used to evade tax.[^ct-rbi-2026]

## Regulation

### Anti-money laundering (FIU-IND)

A finance ministry notification of 7 March 2023 brought VDA service providers under the PMLA. This covers exchange between VDAs and fiat, transfers, custody, and services for token offerings.[^pib-2023] Such providers must register with the Financial Intelligence Unit–India (FIU-IND). They must verify customers and report suspicious transactions.[^pib-2023][^fiu]

The obligation is "activity-based". It applies to providers serving Indian users whether or not they have an office in India.[^pib-2026] FIU-IND has enforced it against offshore exchanges:

- **December 2023:** show-cause notices to nine offshore providers, including Binance, KuCoin, Kraken and Bitfinex. FIU-IND asked the IT ministry to block their websites.[^pib-2023]
- **2024–2025:** penalties on Binance (₹18.82 crore) and Bybit (₹9.27 crore) after they registered.[^inc42-binance][^pib-bybit]
- **2025–2026:** notices to 25 and then 15 more providers. These included takedown orders under the Information Technology Act, 2000.[^pib-2025][^pib-2026]

FIU-IND also warns that "Crypto products and NFTs are unregulated and can be highly risky", with possibly no regulatory recourse.[^pib-2026]

### The RBI's position

The RBI has opposed private cryptocurrencies for years. Its 6 April 2018 circular barred banks and other regulated entities from dealing in virtual currencies or serving firms that did.[^rbi-2018] After the Supreme Court set it aside, a 31 May 2021 circular told banks to stop citing it. Banks still had to apply KYC, AML and foreign-exchange rules.[^rbi-2021]

The RBI runs its own central bank digital currency, the **e₹**, with a retail pilot since December 2022.[^rbi-cbdc] In July 2026, reports based on Reuters said the RBI had told a parliamentary committee it favoured prohibiting private cryptocurrencies.[^ct-rbi-2026]

### The 2020 Supreme Court judgment

In *Internet and Mobile Association of India v. Reserve Bank of India*, decided on 4 March 2020, the Supreme Court set aside the 2018 circular.[^sc-2020] The court accepted that the RBI had power to act against risks to the financial system. It held the measure disproportionate, partly because the RBI had not shown harm to the entities it regulates.[^sc-2020]

### Pending bills and policy

- A 2019 inter-ministerial committee, chaired by Finance Secretary Subhash Chandra Garg, proposed the *Banning of Cryptocurrency and Regulation of Official Digital Currency Bill, 2019*. It would have banned mining, holding and trading private cryptocurrencies. It was never enacted.[^dea-2019]
- The *Cryptocurrency and Regulation of Official Digital Currency Bill, 2021* was listed for Parliament's winter session in 2021. It was never introduced.[^dh-2021]
- As of October 2026, a promised government **discussion paper** on crypto policy has not been published. Parliament's Standing Committee on Finance is studying VDAs.[^ct-rbi-2026]

## Common misunderstandings

- **"Crypto is illegal in India."** It is not banned. It is taxed and subject to AML law.[^pib-2023][^schedule-vda]
- **"Paying tax makes crypto regulated."** Taxing income does not create investor protection. FIU-IND states that crypto products are unregulated.[^pib-2026]
- **"Losses reduce my tax."** VDA losses cannot be set off or carried forward.[^ita-2025]

## Practical notes

- Keep records of each acquisition and transfer, because Schedule VDA asks for them per transaction.[^schedule-vda]
- TDS withheld by an exchange is credited against the seller's final tax, so it should appear in their tax credit statement.[^cbdt-13]
- Section numbers changed on 1 April 2026. Transactions before that date fall under the 1961 Act's sections; later ones under the 2025 Act.[^ita-2025-wiki]
