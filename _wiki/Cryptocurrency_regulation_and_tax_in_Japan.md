> **In plain terms:** Japan was one of the first countries to license crypto exchanges, after the collapse of Mt. Gox in Tokyo. Exchanges must register with the Financial Services Agency and keep most customer coins offline. In July 2026 Parliament voted to regulate crypto like stocks and bonds. Crypto profits are currently taxed as ordinary income at up to about 55%, but a flat 20% rate is planned from 2028.

Japan regulates crypto-assets (暗号資産, *angō shisan*) through the **Financial Services Agency** (FSA). Since April 2017 crypto exchanges have had to register under the **Payment Services Act** (PSA).[^mofo-2019][^fsa-register] The rules were tightened after two large thefts from Japanese exchanges, Coincheck in 2018 and DMM Bitcoin in 2024.[^coincheck][^fbi-dmm]

On 15 July 2026 the National Diet passed amendments moving crypto trading under the **Financial Instruments and Exchange Act** (FIEA), the law that governs securities.[^coindesk-2026][^fsa-diet] Separately, lawmakers approved a plan to tax crypto gains at a flat 20% from 2028, down from rates of up to about 55%.[^coindesk-2026]

## Regulation

### Exchange registration under the Payment Services Act

After Mt. Gox, Japan amended the PSA in 2016. From 1 April 2017, businesses exchanging virtual currency for customers had to register with the FSA.[^mofo-2019] As of October 2026 the FSA's register listed about 21 crypto-asset exchange service providers.[^fsa-register][^takame]

Amendments effective on 1 May 2020 renamed "virtual currency" as **crypto-asset**. They added custody providers to the rules and required firms to keep customers' crypto offline in cold wallets, separate from their own. Misleading advertising was banned.[^mofo-2020][^cfi]

### Stablecoins

From June 2023 the PSA treats fiat-backed stablecoins as **electronic payment instruments**. Only banks, fund-transfer businesses and trust companies may issue them. Intermediaries need registration.[^cfi]

### Two thefts that shaped the rules

- **Coincheck (2018).** Hackers stole about ¥58 billion of NEM coins on 26 January 2018. The coins were in an internet-connected hot wallet, without multi-signature protection, and Coincheck was not yet registered.[^coincheck] The FSA ordered it to improve risk management and inspected other exchanges.[^coincheck] Coincheck promised to repay about 260,000 affected customers in cash.[^coincheck]
- **DMM Bitcoin (2024).** In May 2024, 4,502.9 BTC, about $308 million, was stolen. The FBI, the US Defense Department's Cyber Crime Center and Japan's National Police Agency attributed it to the North Korean group "TraderTraitor".[^fbi-dmm] A fake recruiter on LinkedIn tricked an employee of Ginco, a wallet-software firm, into running malicious code. The attackers then altered a legitimate DMM transaction request.[^fbi-dmm]

### The 2026 move to the FIEA

The bill passed on 15 July 2026 treats buying and selling crypto-assets as a business as a **financial instruments business** under the FIEA.[^takame][^fsa-diet] Its main changes:[^coindesk-2026]

- **insider-trading** rules for crypto-assets;
- regular **disclosure** by token issuers and stricter disclosure and investor protection at exchanges;
- higher penalties for unregistered operators: up to **10 years in prison**, up from 3, and fines up to ¥10 million, up from ¥3 million.

The higher penalties start 20 days after the law is promulgated. Most other provisions start on a date set by Cabinet Order within a year.[^takame] The law also removes a legal obstacle to spot crypto exchange-traded funds, though none have been approved.[^coindesk-2026] Stablecoins remain under the PSA.[^coindesk-2026]

## Taxation

### Current rules: miscellaneous income

The National Tax Agency (NTA) treats gains from selling or exchanging crypto-assets as **miscellaneous income** (雑所得) for most individuals.[^nta-faq] This is added to other income and taxed at progressive rates. National income tax reaches 45% and local inhabitant tax adds 10%, a combined top rate of about 55%.[^coindesk-2026] Losses on crypto generally cannot be set against other kinds of income.[^nta-faq]

Rewards from staking, mining and lending are also generally miscellaneous income when received.[^nta-faq] The NTA's FAQ explains how to calculate cost using the moving-average or total-average method.[^nta-faq]

### Planned change: separate 20% taxation

Lawmakers approved a plan to tax crypto gains at a flat **20%**, split into 15% national and 5% local tax. It is expected to apply from 2028.[^coindesk-2026] The flat rate would match that on listed shares. Reports say it is expected to cover assets traded through FSA-registered businesses.[^coindesk-2026]

## Criticism and open debates

- **Tax level.** Under current rules gains can be taxed at up to about 55%, with losses not offset against other income. The planned 20% rate would put crypto on the same footing as shares.[^coindesk-2026][^nta-faq]
- **Timing.** The tax cut lags the regulatory change by about a year, so traders face the old rates until 2028.[^coindesk-2026]
- **Security.** Both large thefts came from social engineering and operational lapses, not cryptography. The DMM case involved a third-party software provider.[^fbi-dmm]
- **ETFs.** The FSA said it would consider a framework for crypto ETFs, but no product has been approved.[^coindesk-2026]

## Practical notes

- Use FSA-registered exchanges; the FSA publishes the register.[^fsa-register]
- Keep records of each trade, because miscellaneous income must be calculated per year using the NTA's methods.[^nta-faq]
- The 2026 law's exact start dates depend on a Cabinet Order not yet issued as of October 2026.[^takame]
- This article is general information, not tax or legal advice.
