> **In plain terms:** Bitcoin was launched as money that works without banks. Since 2020, fund managers, listed companies and governments have started holding it. People disagree on whether that strengthens Bitcoin or undermines what it was built for.

The **Bitcoin institutionalization debate** is the dispute over Bitcoin's adoption by regulated funds, public companies and governments. It covers three main developments. These are US-listed spot [Bitcoin](../Bitcoin/) exchange-traded products, corporate treasury purchases, and state-level reserves or legal-tender laws. This article sets out the dated events and the positions that named people and institutions have published about them.

## Background

The Bitcoin white paper, published in 2008 under the name [Satoshi Nakamoto](../Satoshi_Nakamoto/), framed the system against reliance on financial intermediaries. Its introduction says online commerce "has come to rely almost exclusively on financial institutions serving as trusted third parties".[^whitepaper]

The first block, mined in January 2009, embeds a newspaper headline: "The Times 03/Jan/2009 Chancellor on brink of second bailout for banks". The string is hard-coded in Bitcoin Core's chain parameters.[^genesis]

Critics of institutional adoption often cite this origin. Supporters reply that the protocol is permissionless, so anyone can hold bitcoin, including institutions. The sections below attribute each of these arguments to a named source.

## Spot exchange-traded products (United States)

An **exchange-traded product** (ETP) is a security, listed on a stock exchange, whose value tracks an underlying asset. A **spot** bitcoin ETP holds bitcoin itself, rather than futures contracts. For listing, an exchange must file a proposed rule change with the SEC under Rule **19b-4**.

### Grayscale v. SEC (2023)

The SEC had rejected a series of spot bitcoin ETP proposals, including Grayscale's request to convert its trust into an ETP. On 29 August 2023, the US Court of Appeals for the D.C. Circuit vacated that rejection. It held that the SEC had not adequately explained why it approved bitcoin futures ETPs but not Grayscale's spot product.[^grayscale]

### Approval order (10 January 2024)

On 10 January 2024, the SEC approved the 19b-4 filings of NYSE Arca, Nasdaq and Cboe BZX. The approval let eleven spot bitcoin ETPs list.[^sec-order] The commissioners published opposing statements the same day.

- **Chair Gary Gensler** voted to approve. He wrote that the Commission "did not approve or endorse bitcoin" and called it "primarily a speculative, volatile asset". He cited the *Grayscale* ruling as the reason approval was "the most sustainable path forward".[^gensler]
- **Commissioner Hester Peirce** voted to approve and criticised the delay. "We squandered a decade of opportunities to do our job," she wrote. She added that the only material change since earlier denials "was a judicial rebuke".[^peirce]
- **Commissioner Caroline Crenshaw** dissented. She called the decision "unsound and ahistorical" and said it could "further sacrifice investor protection".[^crenshaw]

### Custody and forks

Spot ETPs hold bitcoin through a **custodian**, a regulated firm that stores the private keys. The iShares Bitcoin Trust, sponsored by BlackRock, names Coinbase Custody Trust Company as its bitcoin custodian.[^ibit]

Its prospectus says the custodian does not support forked protocols unless Coinbase publicly announces support. It also says the trust will abandon any forked assets it receives.[^ibit] A **fork** here means a split of the blockchain into competing versions (see [Soft fork and hard fork](../Soft_fork_and_hard_fork/)).

### Published positions on ETPs

- **Jameson Lopp** is a Bitcoin developer and co-founder of Casa. In January 2024 he told *The Block* that concentrated custody leaves "relatively few 'doors'" for government agencies to knock on.[^lopp]
- **Arthur Hayes**, co-founder of BitMEX, argued in his December 2023 essay "Expression" that ETFs could harm Bitcoin if they become too successful. He wrote that bitcoin "exists only if it moves". If funds held most coins and never moved them, he argued, fees would fall and miners could not pay for security.[^hayes]
- **Ulrich Bindseil and Jürgen Schaaf** work in the European Central Bank's market infrastructure division. On 22 February 2024, they wrote that ETP approval did not make bitcoin suitable for payments or investment. They maintained that its fair value is zero. The ECB blog states that these are the authors' own views.[^ecb-blog]

## Corporate treasuries

A **corporate treasury** strategy means a company holds bitcoin on its balance sheet in place of, or alongside, cash. US-listed companies disclose material purchases in SEC filings such as the **Form 8-K** (current report) and **Form 10-K** (annual report).

| Company | Filing | Date | Disclosure |
|---|---|---|---|
| MicroStrategy (now Strategy) | Form 8-K | 11 August 2020 | Bought 21,454 bitcoin for $250 million, including fees.[^mstr-8k] |
| Tesla | Form 10-K (FY2020) | 8 February 2021 | Invested an aggregate $1.50 billion in bitcoin. Expected to accept bitcoin as payment.[^tesla-10k] |
| Semler Scientific | Form 8-K | 28 May 2024 | Board adopted bitcoin as primary treasury reserve asset. Bought 581 bitcoin for about $40 million.[^semler-8k] |
| GameStop | Form 8-K | 28 May 2025 | Announced purchase of 4,710 bitcoin.[^gme-8k] |

In the press release attached to MicroStrategy's 8-K, chief executive **Michael Saylor** called bitcoin "a dependable store of value". He said it "can be superior to cash". He cited quantitative easing and pandemic-era uncertainty as threats to the real value of fiat reserves.[^mstr-8k]

Critics such as Bindseil and Schaaf reject the store-of-value premise. They argue that a renewed boom-bust cycle would redistribute wealth "at the expense of the less sophisticated".[^ecb-blog]

## Asset managers

**Larry Fink**, chief executive of BlackRock, addressed bitcoin in his 2025 annual chairman's letter. He wrote that the dollar's reserve status is "not guaranteed to last forever". He warned that, if US debt keeps rising, "America risks losing that position to digital assets like Bitcoin".[^fink-2025] BlackRock is also the sponsor of the iShares Bitcoin Trust.[^ibit]

## El Salvador

### Legal tender (2021)

El Salvador adopted bitcoin as legal tender in September 2021, according to the International Monetary Fund (IMF).[^imf-2022] **Legal tender** is money that must be accepted to settle debts.

In January 2022, the IMF Executive Board said legal-tender status "entails large risks for financial and market integrity". It urged the government to narrow the law's scope by removing that status. Some directors also raised concerns about Bitcoin-backed bonds.[^imf-2022]

### Amendment and IMF programme (2025)

On 29 January 2025, the Legislative Assembly amended the Bitcoin Law. The reform made acceptance voluntary, limited bitcoin use to private parties, and repealed the article allowing taxes to be paid in bitcoin.[^efe-2025]

On 26 February 2025, the IMF Board approved a 40-month Extended Fund Facility of about US$1.4 billion. The IMF listed as completed reforms "making acceptance of Bitcoin voluntary" and "ensuring that tax payments are made only in U.S. dollars". The programme also confines public-sector purchases of bitcoin.[^imf-2025]

### Disputed status

Whether bitcoin remains legal tender is disputed. The amendment removed the legal-tender references from the law. Yet Stacy Herbert, director of the National Bitcoin Office, told reporters that bitcoin would remain legal tender.[^efe-2025]

On 1 October 2026, the IMF completed its second and third programme reviews. It granted "waivers ... for certain unmet performance criteria on Bitcoin accumulation". It stated that "no further Bitcoin accumulation is envisaged beyond the documented donations".[^imf-2026] As of October 2026, majority ownership of the state Chivo wallet had passed to a private operator.[^imf-2026]

## United States Strategic Bitcoin Reserve

### Legislation proposed (2024–2025)

On 31 July 2024, **Senator Cynthia Lummis** introduced the BITCOIN Act. It proposed a federal programme to buy 1 million bitcoin over a fixed period, funded from existing Federal Reserve and Treasury funds.[^lummis-2024] She reintroduced a revised bill on 11 March 2025, describing it as a way to codify the executive order below.[^lummis-2025] As of October 2026, the bill has not become law.

### Executive Order 14233 (6 March 2025)

On 6 March 2025, President Donald Trump signed Executive Order 14233, "Establishment of the Strategic Bitcoin Reserve and United States Digital Asset Stockpile".[^eo-14233] Its main terms are:

- The reserve is funded with bitcoin the Treasury holds through final **forfeiture**, the legal seizure of assets linked to crimes.[^eo-14233]
- "Government BTC deposited into the Strategic Bitcoin Reserve shall not be sold."[^eo-14233]
- Any strategy to acquire more bitcoin must be "budget neutral" and impose no incremental cost on taxpayers.[^eo-14233]
- The order justifies itself by saying "there is a fixed supply of BTC" and that bitcoin is often called "digital gold".[^eo-14233]

The order is a policy directive, not a statute. It applies "subject to the availability of appropriations".[^eo-14233] Gensler's January 2024 description of bitcoin as speculative and linked to illicit finance contrasts with the order's framing of bitcoin as a strategic asset.[^gensler][^eo-14233]

## Points of disagreement

The positions above cluster around four questions. Each claim is attributed to its source.

- **Custody.** Lopp argued that a few custodians create points of state pressure.[^lopp] The iShares prospectus shows that one custodian's fork policy governs the trust's treatment of forks.[^ibit]
- **Network security.** Hayes argued that coins held long-term in funds would reduce fees paid to miners.[^hayes]
- **Value.** Saylor described bitcoin as a store of value superior to cash.[^mstr-8k] Bindseil and Schaaf put its fair value at zero.[^ecb-blog] Gensler called it speculative and volatile.[^gensler]
- **State role.** El Salvador's government moved away from mandatory use under IMF conditions.[^imf-2025] The US government moved toward holding bitcoin as a reserve.[^eo-14233]
