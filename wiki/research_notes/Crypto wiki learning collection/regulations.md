# Global cryptocurrency regulation

## How should beginners understand regulatory layers?
### Takeaway
Rules depend on jurisdiction, activity and legal classification; registration is not an investment endorsement.
### Cited Findings
- FATF standards ask countries to license/register and supervise virtual asset service providers. Providers conduct customer due diligence, keep records, report suspicious transactions, and securely transmit originator/beneficiary information under the Travel Rule. FATF standards require domestic implementation; FATF is not a worldwide crypto licensing authority. [FATF](https://www.fatf-gafi.org/en/topics/virtual-assets.html)
- On March 17, 2026 the SEC issued an interpretation, effective March 23, concerning crypto assets and transactions; CFTC supplied accompanying guidance. It is an interpretive release, not a new Act of Congress or court judgment. [Release](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2026/03/s7-2026-09)
- The interpretation distinguishes digital commodities, collectibles, tools, stablecoins and digital securities. A non-security asset can nevertheless be offered through an investment contract; the transaction and associated promises matter. [SEC explanation](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-30-sec-clarifies-application-federal-securities-laws-crypto-assets)
- January 10, 2024 spot bitcoin ETP listing/trading approvals did not endorse bitcoin or approve every crypto investment. [SEC statement](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/gensler-statement-spot-bitcoin-011023)
- MiCA's maximum EU transitional period expired July 1, 2026. ESMA's April 17 statement says providers without necessary authorization must cease services; sector-specific exemptions and permissions still need legal analysis. Do not describe grandfathering as ongoing in an October 2026 article. [ESMA](https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2026-04/ESMA75-113276571-1679_Statement_on_the_end_of_transitional_periods_under_MiCA.pdf)
- The FCA page updated September 30, 2026 says the new UK regime is expected to commence October 25, 2027; applications are open. Rules published June 30, 2026 should not be presented as already fully operational. [FCA](https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/new-regime-cryptoasset-regulation)
- India's FIU publishes VDA reporting-entity AML/CFT guidelines updated January 8, 2026 and registration circular revised September 15, 2025. [FIU](https://fiuindia.gov.in/files/downloads/downloads.html)
- Singapore's MAS regulates payment-token services under its payments framework and securities-like products under relevant capital-market laws. [MAS](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2025/mas-clarifies-regulatory-regime-for-digital-token-service-providers)
- Hong Kong centralized virtual-asset platforms serving or actively marketing to Hong Kong require SFC licensing under the applicable regime; licence applicants are distinct from licensed entities. [SFC](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/api/circular/list-content/circular/doc?lang=EN&refNo=23EC27)
### Inferences
- Explain separate questions: may a business operate; may it market a product; what disclosures/custody safeguards apply; what does the individual owe in tax. One approval does not answer the others.
- Explain document hierarchy: legislation creates legal rules; agency rules implement mandates; guidance interprets; complaints allege; settlements resolve particular proceedings; court decisions have a jurisdiction and procedural posture. Avoid treating an enforcement allegation as a proven finding.
### Gaps
- No exhaustive review of all 2026 statutes or court rulings was performed. Do not claim complete global coverage. No current litigation-status claim about Ripple, Coinbase or Binance is established by these notes.

## What tax concepts and examples are safe to explain?
### Takeaway
A taxable disposal can happen without cash withdrawal; reward income and later gains can be separate events. Rates and loss relief differ.
### Cited Findings
- US digital assets are property for federal tax purposes; taxable transactions can include sales, exchanges, payments and receipts for work or rewards. Records should identify dates, units, value and basis; tax consequences do not depend solely on whether a broker sends a form. [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/filing/digital-assets)
- UK guidance identifies selling, exchanging one cryptoasset for another, spending, and most gifts as potential disposals for Capital Gains Tax; spouse/civil-partner and charity exceptions require their own conditions. [HMRC](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/check-if-you-need-to-pay-tax-when-you-sell-cryptoassets)
- India's official Schedule VDA guide, updated May 18, 2026, describes transfer income taxed at 30% plus applicable surcharge and cess, with acquisition cost allowed but other expenses/allowances/loss set-offs excluded. [Income Tax Department](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/schedule_vda)
### Inferences
- Hypothetical only: purchase for 100 currency units and disposal for 150 produces a 50-unit gain before fees and jurisdiction-specific adjustments. A swap can constitute the disposal. Do not imply a universal rate or loss deduction.
- Preserve transaction hashes plus exchange exports, fiat valuations, cost records, reward receipts and transfer records. A self-transfer must be distinguishable from a sale; chain records alone do not explain beneficial ownership.
### Gaps
- India's TDS thresholds and interaction with the 2025 Income-tax Act were not fully verified; use official updated guidance before giving section numbering or numerical thresholds. No single global tax treatment of staking, bridging, wrapping, liquidity pools, gifts or airdrops should be asserted.
