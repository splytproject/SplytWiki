**{{ site.title }}** is a free online encyclopedia about cryptocurrency, blockchain technology, decentralized finance and the people and ideas behind them. It is written to be read by anyone, from complete beginners to developers, and it is free to read with no account or paywall.

## Our aims

{{ site.title }} tries to explain crypto the way a good encyclopedia explains any other subject: clearly, neutrally and with sources. The field is full of marketing, speculation and jargon. We aim to cut through that and describe what things are, how they work, where they came from and what their critics say.

## How articles are written

Every article on {{ site.title }} follows a few simple principles:

- **Neutral point of view.** Articles describe projects, people and events fairly and without promotion. Significant criticisms and risks are included alongside benefits.
- **Verifiability.** Facts are supported by reliable sources, such as original white papers, technical specifications, academic research, court records and reputable journalism. Sources are listed in each article's *References* section.
- **No price talk.** Articles avoid live prices, market capitalisations and predictions, because they go out of date within hours and are not what an encyclopedia is for.
- **No paid content.** Projects cannot pay to be covered, to have an article changed or to have criticism removed.

## Not financial advice

{{ site.title }} is an educational reference. Nothing on this site is financial, investment, legal or tax advice. Cryptoassets are highly volatile and you can lose all of the money you put into them. Please read our [general disclaimer](../disclaimer/).
