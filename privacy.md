This policy explains what information is collected when you visit {{ site.title }}. In short: {{ site.title }} itself does not ask for, collect or sell any personal information, but the companies that host the site may process some data as described below.

## Information {{ site.title }} collects

{{ site.title }} has no user accounts, comment forms or newsletter, and it does not set its own tracking or advertising cookies.

**Appearance settings.** If you change the text size, page width or colour theme, your choice is saved in your own browser's local storage so it applies on your next visit. This information never leaves your device, and you can remove it by clearing your browser's site data.

**Search.** Searches run entirely inside your browser. What you type into the search box is not sent to {{ site.title }}.

## Hosting

{{ site.title }} is hosted on **GitHub Pages**, a service of GitHub, Inc. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information about visits, such as IP addresses, for security and to keep the service running. See the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Scripts from jsDelivr

Some technical articles show mathematical formulas or diagrams. Only on those pages, your browser loads the open-source libraries KaTeX and Mermaid from **jsDelivr**, a public content delivery network. jsDelivr receives the usual technical information any web server sees, such as your IP address. It sets no cookies. See the [jsDelivr privacy policy](https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net).

## External links

Articles link to outside sources such as white papers and news sites. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

## Children

{{ site.title }} is a general-audience reference and is not directed at children.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the bottom of this page shows when it was last changed.
