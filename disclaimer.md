## Risk warning

Cryptoassets are highly volatile and largely unregulated in many countries. Their value can fall to zero. Exchanges, wallets and smart contracts can fail, be hacked or turn out to be fraudulent, and transactions usually cannot be reversed. Only use money you can afford to lose completely.

## Information may be out of date

Crypto changes quickly. Software is upgraded, projects are abandoned, laws change and companies collapse. An article may describe a situation that has since changed. The "last edited" date at the bottom of each page shows when it was last updated.
